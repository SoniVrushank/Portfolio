campaign_check.sql
-- which channel actually converts this week? SELECT platform, ROUND(100.0 * clicks / impressions, 2) AS ctr, ROUND(revenue / NULLIF(spend, 0), 2) AS roas FROM ad_campaigns GROUP BY platform ORDER BY roas DESC;

In plain English: a check like this shows whether Meta or Google is really earning its budget this week — before the money gets spent finding out the hard way.
